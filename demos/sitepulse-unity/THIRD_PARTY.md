# Third-party content in Sitepulse

Every asset in this project that was not made for it is listed here, with where it came from
and the license it was used under. Only assets whose license allows redistribution in this
repository are accepted: CC0 / public domain by preference, or an equally permissive license.
Purchase rights alone (for example an asset store license) are not enough.

Reference material used while modelling (photos, drawings, specification sheets) is never
committed, and is not listed here.

The ambientCG materials are re-encoded by `ArtSource/textures/prepare_textures.py` (smaller JPEGs,
and the occlusion, displacement and roughness maps packed into one terrain mask map); the
pixels are otherwise unchanged. ambientCG states that all of its assets are released under
CC0 1.0 Universal: https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/

## Packages

their own licenses; none are vendored into this repository.
