# User guide

This guide walks through the DeviceChain web console, screen by screen, in the order the
console's own navigation presents them. Each chapter explains what a screen is for, when you
would use it, and what to expect, and is illustrated with screenshots of the console as shipped.

The guide is written for two audiences:

- **Tenant users and administrators**, who work with the devices, rules, alarms and dashboards
  of one tenant. Most chapters are for you.
- **Instance operators**, who administer the whole installation: tenants, tiers, identities and
  identity providers. Those screens are gathered in their own chapter.

New to DeviceChain? Start with [Your First Device](../quickstart/first-device.md) to get data
flowing, then come back here to learn the console. For how the pieces fit together, see the
[Concepts](../concepts/architecture.md) section.

## Chapters

Chapters are published as they are completed.

| # | Chapter | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Signing in | The sign-in steps, single sign-on, choosing a tenant, an expired session, and the user menu (theme, language, profile). |
| 2 | The console at a glance | The home page, the sidebar, the tenant chip, and what you see when an area is unavailable. |
| 3 | Devices | Listing, creating and importing devices; the device detail tabs, including assignment, connectivity and location, events, commands, configuration, credentials and replacement. |
| 4 | Device types and profiles | Device types and versioned device profiles: metrics, commands, configuration, and publishing or rolling back a version. |
| 5 | Detection rules | Authoring rules as a form, on the canvas, or in plain language; replay preview; rule health and the live feed. |
| 6 | Alarms | The live alarm list, filters, acknowledging and clearing. |
| 7 | Commanding a fleet | Command batches: creating one, following its progress, refusals and cancellation. |
| 8 | Dashboards | Templates and import, viewing, editing on the canvas, widgets and bindings, export, and version history. |
| 9 | Assets, customers and areas | The registry families: lists, hierarchy, property schemas and members. |
| 10 | Geofences | Drawing and editing fences, and their history. |
| 11 | Groups, facets and Browse | Facet keys, the Browse query builder, and saving a result as a dynamic group. |
| 12 | Connectors | Outbound connectors: creating, configuring and versioning them. |
| 13 | Audit | The tenant audit journal, filters and export. |
| 14 | Tenant settings | Branding, map and language. |
| 15 | Administering the instance | Tenants, deletions, tiers, identities, roles, identity providers, AI providers, instance audit and settings. |
| 16 | Updates (over the air) | Coming later. The console for firmware and software updates has not shipped yet. |
| 17 | Viewing dashboards in /dash | The read-only dashboard viewer: signing in and loading a dashboard by token. |
